Icons in `hicolor/scalable/actions` are Lucide drawings (ISC), matching
lucide-react used by https://dev-null.sh, plus the GitHub mark from the
web client.

`devnull-mark.svg`, `devnull-mark-dark.svg`, and the two slashes in
`sh.devnull.Client.svg` are the official Mark from
https://dev-null.sh/brand (`dev-null-mark-light.svg` /
`dev-null-mark-dark.svg`). `pastel-de-nata.svg` is copied from the web
app `public/` assets.
